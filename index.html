ABOUT PROJECT

What is Voter Awareness?

Voter awareness means educating citizens about voting rights, responsibilities and the election process. Digital platforms such as websites, social media and online videos can help people access useful and verified information easily.

📚

Education

Provide simple information about the voting process and voter responsibilities.

📢

Awareness

Spread voter awareness through digital platforms and social media.

🔎

Verification

Encourage citizens to check election information from reliable official sources.
